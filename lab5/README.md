# Lab 5: Architecture (Component Diagram)

Domain: **Gym** (same as in Lab 1 to Lab 4)

## Description:

UML component diagram of the gym system: components, provided and required interfaces, dependencies, system boundary and external integrations. Every component traces to the Lab 1 data model, Lab 2 requirements, Lab 3 interaction and Lab 4 process.

## Files:

- `spec.md`: modules, interfaces, allowed dependencies, external integrations and acceptance criteria
- `diagrams/`: component diagram (PlantUML source and PNG render)
- `adr/`: architecture decision records (MADR)
- `ai/`: AI prompts, raw AI responses and decisions after gap analysis
- `audit.md`: audit of discrepancies, traceability and AI errors
- `DEFENSE.md`: defense notes
