# Lab 4: Process (Activity Diagram)

Domain: **Gym** (same as in Lab 1, Lab 2 and Lab 3)

## Description:

Activity diagram of one business process, the purchase of a membership plan (UC-07 from Lab 2), with swimlanes, a decision and a parallel branch. Roles correspond to actors from Lab 2, data to entities from the Lab 1 ER model.

## Files:

- `spec.md`: process, roles, steps, branches and acceptance criteria
- `diagrams/`: activity diagram (PlantUML source and PNG render)
- `adr/`: architecture decision records (MADR)
- `ai/`: AI prompts and raw AI responses
